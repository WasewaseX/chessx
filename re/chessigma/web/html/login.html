Free Chess Analysis for Every Game

Chessigma is a free chess analyzer that runs on your device. Enter your Chess.com or Lichess username, pick a game, and get a full chess analysis report in seconds. No daily limit, no premium tier, and no signup required to start analyzing.

Every move gets a grade from Brilliant to Blunder based on how much it changed your chances. The report shows your overall accuracy, identifies the opening you played, highlights your best and worst moves, and gives you the engine top choice for every position. It is the same kind of free chess game review that Chess.com locks behind a premium subscription.

What Your Game Report Shows

The chess game analysis breaks down every move you played. Each move gets a quality grade so you can see exactly where you gained or lost advantage. The evaluation graph plots the entire game so you can spot the turning points at a glance.

You can step through the game on the chess analysis board, toggle engine lines, and explore the variations you missed. Openings are identified automatically so you know what you played and where you left theory, and when a game exposes a hole in your repertoire you can drill the exact line in our free chess openings trainer. The chess game analyzer also highlights your best move and your biggest mistake, making it easy to focus your review. If you just want a single position checked, our free chess AI returns the Stockfish best move from any FEN in seconds.

Works with Chess.com, Lichess and PGN

Type your Chess.com or Lichess username above and your recent games load automatically. If you have games saved as PGN files, paste the text or upload the file directly.

On Chess.com, free users get one game analysis per day and need a Diamond membership for full engine review. On Chessigma, you can review every game in your history with full Stockfish analysis for free. Blitz, rapid, bullet, classical. No restrictions on time control or number of games. Curious how a win or loss will move your rating? Try our chess Elo calculator to estimate rating changes before your next match.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chessigma really free?

Yes, completely. There is no paywall and no daily game limit. You can create an account to save your usernames and access puzzles, but it is not required to analyze. The analysis runs on your own device, which is how we keep it free and unlimited for everyone.

How is this different from Chess.com analysis?

Chess.com gives free users one game review per day and locks full engine analysis behind a premium subscription. Chessigma removes those limits. You get unlimited game reviews with every move graded, your accuracy, the best move in every position, and a complete report every time.

What does the analysis report show?

Every move gets a grade from Brilliant down to Blunder, based on how much it changed your chances of winning. You also get an overall accuracy percentage, the opening you played, an evaluation graph that shows how the advantage shifted, and the engine best move for every position. You can explore all of this on the analysis board.

Do I need to create an account?

Not to analyze. Type your Chess.com or Lichess username, or paste a PGN, and you can start right away. Creating an account lets you save your usernames, track multiple players, and access puzzles, but the analysis works without one.

Which platforms can I import games from?

Chess.com, Lichess, and any standard PGN file. For Chess.com and Lichess, enter your username and your recent games load automatically. For PGN, paste the text or upload a .pgn file.

How accurate is the chess analysis?

Chessigma uses Stockfish, the strongest open-source chess engine in the world and the one behind every major chess site. It grades every move, scores your accuracy and finds the best move in each position, running on your device so it stays free and unlimited.
